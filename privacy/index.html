AIDA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

AIDA is an AI operations assistant for small and mid-sized companies. It connects to the business tools you already use so it can help with finance, scheduling, and record-keeping on your behalf.

This policy explains what information AIDA collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. AIDA is a product operated by Sparkup Sdn Bhd (Company Registration No. 202401041258), a company registered in Malaysia. In this policy, "AIDA," "we," "us," and "our" refer to Sparkup Sdn Bhd.

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — the name, email address, company name, and workspace details you provide when you sign up.
  • Business configuration — settings you enter into AIDA, such as company profile, invoice terms, sender addresses, and the identifiers of documents you ask AIDA to work with.
  • Connected service data — when you connect a third-party account (such as Google, Xero, or QuickBooks), AIDA accesses data from that service to perform the tasks you request. This is described in detail below.
  • Usage information — basic technical logs needed to operate the service securely and diagnose problems.

Google user data

When you choose to connect your Google account, AIDA requests access to the following Google services, and only these:

  • Google Sheets (.../auth/spreadsheets) — to read from and write to the specific spreadsheets you configure in AIDA, for example syncing candidate lists, project trackers, and lead lists.
  • Google Calendar (.../auth/calendar) — to read your availability and create or update calendar events when you ask AIDA to schedule on your behalf.

We also request your basic profile email address so AIDA can display which Google account is connected. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive files beyond the sheets you specify, contacts, or any other Google data.

Access is granted through Google's own secure sign-in screen. AIDA never sees or stores your Google password. You authorize access yourself, and you can revoke it at any time.

Limited Use disclosure. AIDA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in this policy; we do not transfer or sell this data to third parties except as necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; we do not use this data for advertising; and no humans read this data except with your explicit consent, to comply with applicable law, for security purposes, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the features you request, such as reading and updating your spreadsheets or managing calendar events.
  • Authenticate you and keep your workspace secure.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the service.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your connected-service data for advertising.

How your data is stored and protected

Access tokens for connected services are stored securely and separately for each company workspace, so one company's data is never accessible to another. We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest, and we limit access to the systems that hold it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share information only with service providers that help us operate AIDA (for example, cloud hosting and email delivery), and only to the extent needed to run the service. These providers are bound to protect your information. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can disconnect any connected service at any time from AIDA's settings, which removes AIDA's stored access to that service.

To revoke AIDA's access to your Google account directly, you can also visit your Google Account permissions page.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Your choices

  • You choose which services to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • You can close your account by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through the service or by email.
